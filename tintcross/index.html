cross.

Slide tinted glass, overlap shapes and mix new colors. Light every beacon at once — a calm logic puzzle for iPhone and iPad.

Coming soon to theApp Store Free · no in-app purchases

One rule. Endless depth.

Every shape is a pane of tinted glass. Where panes overlap, their colors mix — and each beacon on the board asks for exactly one color.

You can't lose

No timers, no move limit, no game over. Think as long as you like.

Hundreds of levels

Chapters rise from Easy to Super, then let you breathe. New chapters keep coming.

For every eye

Each color has its own pattern — stripes, dots or waves — plus a high-contrast mode.

New ideas each chapter

Paint stains, mixed glass, white glass that flips colors, rails, pivots and twin beacons.

Always solvable

Every level is checked by a solver, with a known best number of moves.

Plays offline

No account and no internet needed to play. A soft melody plays when you solve a level.

Play in your language
